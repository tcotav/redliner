Mira had always known she would leave the harbor town of Selkirk, but she
hadn't expected to leave it running.

She sprinted down the wet cobblestones, her green eyes scanning the alleys
for a place to hide. Behind her, the harbor bell was ringing the alarm,
which meant the customs officers had found the ledger she'd stolen from
her uncle's shop. She was scared. She was very scared, and she felt fear
in her chest as she ran.

Ducking into a fish stall, Mira crouched behind a crate of herring and
waited. Her heart was pounding. It was pounding very hard, like a drum,
and she could hear it pounding in her ears.

When the footsteps passed, she let out the breath she'd been holding and
allowed herself, for the first time in an hour, to think about what came
next. The ledger named every smuggler in Selkirk, including her uncle. If
the customs office got hold of it before she did, half the harbor would be
in chains by morning.

She needed to get to the Gray Gull before it sailed. Captain Renne had
promised her passage out, no questions asked, provided she brought
something worth the risk. The ledger was worth more than Renne knew.
